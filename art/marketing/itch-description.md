# Cyborg Swarm

Cyborg Swarm is a game jam strategy prototype about shaping an evolving machine swarm instead of micromanaging individual units.

Place beacons, manage pressure, spend biomass, and watch small cyber-units compete, merge, evolve, and flood the battlefield. Each unit is simple and partly autonomous, but the swarm as a whole can develop a personality: fast raiders, dense defenders, capture-focused pressure, or risky mutations that pay off only if the colony survives long enough.

In this jam build:

- Guide autonomous cyber-units across compact sci-fi battlefields
- Capture enemy bases while defending your own territory
- Use biomass, upgrades, evolution, and fusion to reshape the swarm
- Watch captains organize squads and pull units toward tactical priorities
- Explore readable emergent battles built from simple rules

This is an experimental jam build focused on swarm behavior, strategic nudges, and visible evolution rather than a finished campaign.
